Engagement score:

Weighted by Engagement Quality

The multipliers reflect how much effort each action requires:
    Retweets (2x weight): Highest effort. someone is vouching for the content to their entire audience
    Likes (1x weight): Medium effort. shows approval but less commitment
    Replies (0.5x weight): Can be positive or negative, and sometimes just spam (especially nowadays with lots of bot replies), so weighted lowest
